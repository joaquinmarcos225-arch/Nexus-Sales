Política de privacidad de la extensión

Última actualización: 18 de agosto de 2026

Purpose

Nexus Sales — Outreach Assist connects the Nexus Sales web app to WhatsApp Web so an authenticated seller can open a chat, paste a prepared message, and confirm send with their own click or Enter. The extension does not send messages by itself.

Nexus Sales — Outreach Assist conecta la aplicación Nexus con WhatsApp Web para que un usuario autenticado abra conversaciones, copie mensajes preparados y confirme el envío de forma manual.

Data

  • Nexus company and session identifiers, stored locally in Chrome.
  • Prospect identifiers and phone numbers needed to open WhatsApp Web.
  • Assisted-task status the user chooses to register back in Nexus.
  • Minimal technical logs to diagnose integration errors.

Use and transfer

Data is sent only to the Nexus Sales API to provide the service to the user's company. CostGuard does not sell personal data, does not use it for advertising, and does not transfer it to unrelated third parties.

Browser permissions

The extension requests access to Nexus Sales, its API, and WhatsApp Web. It does not auto-send, scrape inboxes, or install other software. LinkedIn outreach in Nexus uses a human click that opens the profile; this Store package does not read LinkedIn Messaging or connection degree.

Retention

Session data can be removed by signing out of Nexus or uninstalling the extension. Service logs follow the company's contract with CostGuard.
